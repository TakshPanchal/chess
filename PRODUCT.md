# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Casual chess players who want to find an opponent and play without creating an account. The interface should work on desktop and mobile without assuming expert familiarity with chess applications.

## Product Purpose

Provide a clear path from a dedicated welcome screen to guest matchmaking and live two-player chess. Success means players understand how to start, whose turn it is, what happened, and what they can do next.

## Brand Personality

Focused, approachable, dependable. Use direct functional language rather than promotional slogans. Professional means predictable interaction and clear hierarchy, not decorative complexity.

## Anti-references

Avoid toy-like presentation, oversized promotional headings in the playing interface, decorative slogans, dashboard-style card grids, invented statistics, and controls for unsupported features. Lichess is a reference for board-first organization, not a visual template to copy.

## Design Principles

1. Give each screen one clear primary action appropriate to its state.
2. Make the board the dominant element during play; keep supporting information subordinate.
3. Keep game status, move history, and actions in predictable locations.
4. Communicate connecting, searching, active play, disconnection, and game completion distinctly and truthfully.
5. Preserve a dedicated welcome screen and a focused playing screen without redundant start actions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, touch-sized targets, and state cues that do not rely on color alone. Support desktop and mobile layouts and respect reduced-motion preferences.
